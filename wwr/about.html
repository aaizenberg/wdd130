River Current Rafting

We turn a day on the river into a story worth retelling. River Current Rafting pairs local river knowledge, thoughtful preparation, and a genuine love for the outdoors so every guest can paddle with confidence and leave with a bigger smile.

History

River Current Rafting began in 2004 when two lifelong guides set out to share their favorite mountain river with small groups of curious adventurers. What started with one raft and a borrowed trailer has grown into a close-knit crew known for careful guiding, good river stories, and warm welcomes at the put-in.
