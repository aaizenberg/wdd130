Featured runs

Three ways to get on the river

Class II · 3 hours

Half Day Adventure

Ideal for first-time rafters and families. Practice the basics on lively but approachable water, take in the canyon views, and finish the day wanting just one more rapid.

Best for: ages 6+ and groups looking for a relaxed introduction.

Class III · Full day

Big Mallard

A classic full-day rafting experience with splashy waves, active teamwork, a riverside lunch, and enough calm water to admire the forest and trade river stories.

Best for: active families, friends, and paddlers ready to step up the excitement.

Class IV · Full day

Los Dientes del Diablo

For guests who want the river to ask more of them. This demanding run delivers powerful wave trains, precise lines, and a guide-led progression through technical white water.

Best for: confident swimmers seeking a challenging, high-energy river day.
